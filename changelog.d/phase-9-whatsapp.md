### Added

- Link your own WhatsApp account in Marketplace, Apps, WhatsApp. Scan the QR code on the page with your phone (WhatsApp, Settings, Linked devices). Choose the chats that can reach an agent in Allowed chats. Messages from every other chat are dropped. The WhatsApp Orchestrator answers in the same chat. Unlink logs the account out of WhatsApp and deletes its login from this computer.
- WhatsApp uses an unofficial client library, so WhatsApp can block the number. See WhatsApp connections in PRIVACY.md.
